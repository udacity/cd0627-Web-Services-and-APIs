Product Manual: SuperWidget 3000

Setup:
Plug the SuperWidget 3000 into a standard 120V outlet. Do not use 240V outlets as this will damage the device. The blue LED will light up when the device is receiving power.

Calibration:
Press and hold the green button for 3 seconds to start auto-calibration. The device will beep twice when calibration is complete. Recalibrate after moving the device to a new location.

Factory Reset:
To reset the SuperWidget 3000 to factory defaults, press and hold the red and green buttons simultaneously for 5 seconds. All custom settings, Wi-Fi credentials, and saved profiles will be erased. You will need to recalibrate after a factory reset.

Troubleshooting:
If the red light blinks rapidly, the battery is low — connect to a power outlet immediately. If the device is unresponsive, perform a factory reset. If the blue light flashes three times, there is a Wi-Fi connectivity issue — check your router settings.
